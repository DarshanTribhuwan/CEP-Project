I was thinking on creating a self learning and self upgrading ai That's a very ambitious idea, and it's actually one of the biggest open problems in AI research.
However, "self-learning and self-upgrading AI" is too broad to be a practical college research project. You'll need to narrow it to one specific research question.
What do you mean by "self-learning"?

There are different interpretations: 1. Continual (Lifelong) Learning The AI learns new tasks over time without forgetting old ones. Research challenge: catastrophic forgetting. This is an active research area. 2. Self-Improving AI The AI analyzes its own mistakes and updates its behavior automatically. Current systems can do this in limited ways, but safely and reliably is still an open problem. 3. Autonomous AI Agent The AI plans, executes tasks, evaluates results, and improves its strategy. Examples include coding agents that debug their own code. Still an active research field. 4. Recursive Self-Improvement The AI modifies its own architecture or training algorithms to become more intelligent. This is largely theoretical and far beyond what is feasible for a college project.
A realistic research project
Instead of trying to build an AGI, you could build:
"A Continual Learning AI Assistant That Learns New User Preferences Without Forgetting Previous Knowledge"
The system could: Learn new user preferences from conversations. Update its internal memory over time. Avoid catastrophic forgetting. Explain why it made a particular decision. Improve its responses based on user feedback.
This is a genuine research problem because current AI systems still struggle to learn continuously while retaining previous knowledge.
