Platform Features

Everything a farmer needs,
in one place.

Six integrated modules working together — from soil upload to market prices to voice queries in Marathi.

🤖

AI Agronomist

Ask any farming question in natural language. Groq's Llama 3.3 70B model, with live web search, delivers expert agronomic guidance grounded in your actual farm data.

🌱

Soil Health Card Analysis

Upload your government Soil Health Card (PDF or image) directly in the AI chat. SAMPADA extracts pH, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and micronutrients for tailored advice.

🌾

Crop Recommendations

Multi-factor engine weighing soil suitability, monsoon timing, APMC market trends, water availability, and past crop rotation history for each plot.

🎙️

Voice Assistant

Speak naturally in Marathi, Hindi, or English. Real-time speech recognition sends your question to the AI and reads the response aloud — no typing needed.

📜

Farm & Soil History

Chronological timeline of crop seasons, soil tests, and harvest outcomes. Each season's data makes the next recommendation more precise.

📊

Market Intelligence

Live APMC mandi commodity prices, MSP comparisons, and market arrival data for Maharashtra's major agricultural markets.

Simple Process

Up and running in minutes.

1

Register Your Farm

Create a free account with your mobile number, then register your plot with GPS location, area, and irrigation type.

2

Add Soil Data

Upload your Soil Health Card or select your soil type. SAMPADA estimates nutrient profiles using verified soil science models.

3

Get Recommendations

Run the seasonal crop engine to see ranked recommendations with confidence scores, or ask the AI Agronomist directly.

4

Track & Improve

Record your harvest outcomes. Each season's data feeds back into smarter future recommendations for your specific plots.
